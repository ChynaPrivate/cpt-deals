Scroll these yourself — don't let anything automate it

Opening dozens of profiles in quick succession from one account is the pattern Instagram's systems flag. Normal human browsing in the app is fine; automated browsing of a personal account breaches their terms and risks an action block.

When you spot a special: screenshot it, then add it through /admin/capture — same as photographing a board.
