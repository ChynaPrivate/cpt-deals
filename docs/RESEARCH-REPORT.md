# Gardens restaurant research report

**Research date:** 20 August 2026
**Area covered:** the suburb of Gardens, Cape Town — Kloof Street, Kloof Nek Road (Gardens side), Park Road, Orange Street, Buitenkant Street, Hof Street and surrounds.
**Rule applied:** accuracy over volume. An offer is published only when a source that is current today states it. Everything else is recorded here and, where it is a plausible offer, placed in the private research queue.

> **This research is not exhaustive.** Several sources could not be read — see [Sources that could not be accessed](#sources-that-could-not-be-accessed). Instagram and Facebook pages sit behind sign-in walls and were not bypassed, so venues that publish their specials only on social media are almost certainly under-represented here.

### What this means in practice

- Venues that publish specials only on Instagram or Facebook are missing from this dataset.
- No venue has coordinates, so "Nearest" sorting places every venue in the "distance unknown" group until coordinates are added through the admin dashboard. The rest of the site is unaffected.
- Societi Bistro, Upper Union, Blondie, Melissa's, Manna Epicure, Thali, Ocean Basket Gardens and Col'Cacchio Gardens were identified as Gardens or possible-Gardens venues but could not be checked against a working official source in this pass. They are worth a second look by hand.
